## Narration

**Opening — 0:00–0:12**

“A reward rate can look impressive and still leave you worse off. Before spending on a GameFi strategy, check three things: the capital you put at risk, what you can actually realize, and the costs required to exit.”

**Check 1: capital — 0:12–0:36**

“Start with the full entry cost, not just the cheapest item. Include the assets the strategy actually needs. Then separate what is sunk, what might be recovered, and what remains at risk.”

**Check 2: realizable earnings — 0:36–1:00**

“A token reward or points balance is not automatically cash income. Ask whether there is a lawful, repeatable route to claim and sell it, and account for fees, liquidity, and the exit price. If that route cannot be verified, the honest ROI is unavailable—not a made-up number or a zero.”

**Check 3: evidence and risk — 1:00–1:24**

“Look at when the inputs were updated and which values are live observations versus assumptions. Risk and confidence answer different questions: risk is about the possible economic downside; confidence is about how much the data supports the model.”

**Call to action — 1:24–1:40**

“GamCryp’s live comparison shows the modeled strategy, net earnings, break-even, risk, confidence, and data freshness together. Open the link below, and check the calculation time before relying on a result. Estimates are scenario-specific; returns are never guaranteed.”
