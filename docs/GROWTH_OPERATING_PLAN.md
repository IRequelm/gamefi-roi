## Product loop

1. Observe Google Trends and authorized X signals.
2. Persist each new lead with provenance and a one-time approval token.
3. Email the operator a research summary and the exact `SITEYE_EKLE <token>` command.
4. On approval, add a clearly labelled `RESEARCH` candidate page. Do not invent an official URL, reward value, exit path, or ROI.
5. Verify official identity, participation, reward mechanism, and exit evidence.
6. Promote only evidence-backed candidates to GUIDE_ONLY or MODELED status.
7. Generate content from the approved evidence and current GamCryp pages.
8. Require creative review, product visuals, narration metadata, frame QA, and checksum approval before paid audio or external publication.
9. Publish at most two X posts and one YouTube Short per local day, subject to live credentials, quota, caps, and quality gates.
10. Measure acquisition, outbound clicks, verified conversions, verified revenue, and content performance separately from ROI and organic ranking.

## Acquisition measurement contract

- The decision KPI for an organic content probe is **attributed qualified-visit rate**: GA4 sessions carrying that post/video's exact `utm_content` that include an opportunity/strategy view, divided by attributed landing sessions for that `utm_content`. Also inspect `start_click` and `outbound_click` by the same content ID; never treat them as verified conversions or revenue.
- The upstream diagnostic is platform link clicks / impressions when the platform supplies both. The downstream diagnostic is first-party `/go` redirect events split into automated and human-or-unknown traffic. A redirect is not proof that a person converted or that a partner paid.
- GA4's `session_manual_ad_content` dimension already exposes standard session-level `utm_content`, so tagged low-volume tests can be evaluated in GA4 today subject to consent and attribution limits. The additional `utm_content` field in the local first-party landing-event schema and consented PostHog payloads remains a local, undeployed change; verify that separately before relying on first-party/PostHog attribution.
- Do not call channel performance from the current tiny baseline. Treat organic X/Short comparisons as directional, not randomized causal tests. Review weekly; wait for at least 20 attributed landing sessions per variant before comparing qualified-visit rates, and mark smaller samples inconclusive. This is a minimum evidence guard, not a promise of statistical power.
- Explicit `codex_smoke`, `manual`, localhost, and operator-test traffic stays out of qualified organic acquisition counts. Direct traffic remains unattributed unless a source can be established; it must not be relabeled as organic.
- Avoid user/session identity stitching in first-party storage. GA4 session-scoped measures are consent-dependent; first-party landing and redirect totals are event totals and cannot be joined into unique-user funnels without a separately reviewed privacy design.
